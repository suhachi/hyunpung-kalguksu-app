- ✅ 중첩 보호: `RequireAuth` → `RequireAdmin`

**라우트 구조**:
```tsx
<Route path="/login" element={<Login />} />
<Route path="/admin" element={
  <RequireAuth>
    <RequireAdmin>
      <AdminLayout />
    </RequireAdmin>
  </RequireAuth>
}>
  {/* Admin 하위 라우트들 */}
</Route>
```
